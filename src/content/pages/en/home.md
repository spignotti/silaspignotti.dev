## How I work

1. **Understand the business process.** I look at how the work is done today and talk to the people who do it. From this I derive rules, data and special cases.
2. **Plan.** I decide what gets built and what does not, and write it down as a task with acceptance criteria. Decisions that are not visible in the code get a short written record.
3. **Build with agents.** AI coding agents implement the tasks in separate working copies. The rules they follow live in the repository.
4. **Check.** Automated checks, a review and, for visible changes, a test in the running app decide whether a change is merged. I approve risky changes myself.
5. **Run it.** Releases, deploys, monitoring and backups are part of the work. Feedback from daily use becomes new tasks.
